2 · Comp or void, with the manager's PIN

From the table panel, the waiter taps an item and picks Comp (made, on the house) or Void (a mistake, never cooked). A reason is required. The manager approves by entering their PIN on the same tablet, so the waiter doesn't have to go and find their screen. A void on a dish the kitchen hasn't bumped shows up struck through on the kitchen ticket.
