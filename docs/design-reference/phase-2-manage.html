6 · Floor plan editor

Drag tables on a snapping grid, one room at a time. Add a round table, a square one or a booth; names continue the sequence (T17, B6). The inspector sets name, shape, seats and waiter. A table with an open check can't be deleted or renamed until it's closed. Nothing changes for waiters until you press Save.
